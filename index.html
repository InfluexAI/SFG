You Don't Have to Say It Out Loud. We've Heard It Enough Times to Know.

Risk 01

Market Risk.

What if the market drops right when you start drawing income? Unlike your working years, there's no time to recover. A bad sequence of returns early in retirement can permanently alter everything you planned for.

Risk 02

Tax Risk.

Decades of deferred taxes are sitting inside your investments, retirement plans, and deferred compensation, and retirement is often when the IRS comes to collect. Without a proactive strategy, you could hand over far more than necessary during the years you can least afford it.

Risk 03

Income Risk.

The paycheck stops. Now every dollar has to be engineered to last. Without a deliberate income design, you're guessing, and in retirement, guessing has consequences that compound.

These three risks are real. They're what brought you here.

But here's what most people don't realize: there are three more.

The Hidden Risks That Quietly Undermine Even the Best-Laid Plans

Most advisors address what you ask about. We address what you haven't thought to ask yet.

Risk 04

Purpose-Driven Assets.

Not every dollar should do the same job. Without intentional design, assets end up misallocated, working against each other instead of together. The result is unnecessary exposure, missed opportunity, and a financial picture that doesn't match how you actually live.

Risk 05

Liquidity & Opportunity.

Wealth locked in the wrong places creates a different kind of poverty. Whether your wealth is in a business, real estate, or a lifetime of investing, the inability to access capital without disrupting your plan is a risk most people never see coming.

Risk 06

Estate Planning.

This isn't just documents. Without intentional design, what you've spent a lifetime building may not go where you intend, and the cost, delay, and conflict left behind can undo in months what took decades to create.

Six areas. All interconnected. None designed to function alone.

WealthSync is the proprietary wealth management process developed by Brian Skrobonja at Skrobonja Financial Group that coordinates every layer of a client's financial life into one aligned strategy. The process moves through three phases: Discovery and Mapping, in which the client's goals, values, risks, and financial landscape are documented; Design and Implementation, in which a custom plan is built across investments, income, tax, and estate and immediately executed; and Ongoing Optimization, in which the plan is continuously monitored and adjusted as life, goals, and the tax environment change. WealthSync is designed for business owners, executives, and high-income families navigating the transition from wealth accumulation to wealth distribution, protection, and legacy.

Why I've Spent 30 Years Focused on This One Problem

I grew up in a blue-collar family in St. Louis. My dad came from Croatia, worked hard his entire life, and was always the smartest guy in the room when it came to figuring things out. But he traded time for money his whole career, and I watched that limit what was possible for him.

In my twenties I made the same mistake. I chased the lifestyle before I understood how wealth actually works. It took years of doing it wrong before I had the clarity that changed everything: real financial freedom doesn't come from earning more. It comes from building assets that generate income, so money works for you instead of the other way around.

That realization became the foundation for everything I've built since 1993. Thirty-plus years of working alongside successful families and entrepreneurs at exactly the moment that matters most, the transition from building wealth to protecting it, living on it, and passing it on.

What I've learned over those years is simple: the people who struggle in this season aren't the ones who made bad decisions. They're the ones who didn't have a system, or someone coordinating the whole picture.

WealthSync™ is the answer to that problem. And after three decades, I still love this work, because I know what it means to a family when it's done right.

My job isn't to manage your money. It's to make sure you never have to wonder if something important is being missed.
